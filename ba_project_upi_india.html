Problem Statement
The Business Problem We Are Solving

Despite explosive UPI adoption, critical gaps persist in revenue sustainability, inclusion, and fraud management.

💰

Revenue Viability Crisis

NPCI's zero-MDR (Merchant Discount Rate) policy on UPI means payment apps earn nothing on transactions. PhonePe, GPay, and Paytm process billions of transactions with near-zero monetization. How do these platforms stay profitable?

🌾

Last-Mile Financial Exclusion

340 million Indians still lack smartphones. UPI's reach stops at the feature phone boundary. PMJDY accounts exist but remain dormant. Rural Bharat is digitally excluded from the payment revolution despite being the largest demographic.

🛡️

Surging UPI Fraud Ecosystem

Cybercrime losses hit ₹11,000 Cr in FY25. Vishing, SIM-swap, and screen-share frauds are growing 67% YoY. Existing ML models have 18% false positive rates, disrupting legitimate users. RBI has issued 3 emergency circulars in 2025 alone.

🏦

Bank vs. Fintech Power Struggle

Traditional PSBs (SBI, PNB) are losing 40M+ digitally active customers to fintech super-apps. Legacy core banking systems cannot match the UX velocity of fintechs. NPA pressure limits banks' tech investment capacity.

🎯

Project Objective Statement

As a Business Analyst, this project aims to: (1) Quantify the revenue gap in the zero-MDR UPI economy using guestimates, (2) Identify monetization strategies through case study analysis of PhonePe, NPCI's UPI123Pay, and Juspay, (3) Build a data-backed dashboard to support strategic decisions for product, policy, and partnerships stakeholders in the BFSI sector.

Fintech Strategy

PhonePe's Profitability Pivot: From UPI to Financial Superapp

PhonePe Pvt. Ltd. · Bangalore · 2023–2026

Problem: PhonePe held 47% UPI market share but made no money on transactions due to zero-MDR. With $1B+ raised and an IPO approaching, it needed a viable P&L.

BA Solution Path: Analysed user journey data to identify monetizable moments — bill payment (₹3–7 convenience fee), insurance distribution (15–25% commission), gold investments (0.5% spread), and merchant credit EMI (2.5% processing fee).

Key BA Findings

Insurance GWP crossed ₹2,400 Cr by FY25 — highest-margin vertical

Merchant credit product reduces B2B churn by 34% (stickiness metric)

ARPU (Average Revenue Per User) grew from ₹14 to ₹82 over 3 years

Policy & Inclusion

UPI123Pay & UPI Lite: Cracking the Feature Phone Problem

NPCI / RBI · National · 2022–2026

Problem: India has 340 million feature phone users who couldn't access UPI. PMJDY Jan Dhan accounts were dormant — no digital activation pathway. Financial inclusion was stuck.

BA Solution Path: NPCI's product team mapped user journeys for non-smartphone users. IVR-based UPI123Pay was designed for feature phones using USSD-like interactive voice. UPI Lite enables offline small-value payments (≤₹500) stored in on-device wallet — no internet needed.

Key BA Findings

UPI123Pay onboarded 8.4 Mn feature phone users in 18 months

UPI Lite reduces server load by 40% — better uptime, faster txns

Average ticket size via UPI123Pay is ₹340 — purely micro-payment

Infrastructure Tech

Juspay's UPI Deep Tech: Solving the 'Failed Transaction' Problem

Juspay Technologies · Bangalore · 2024–2026

Problem: UPI failure rates were 15–22% during peak traffic (salary days, festive sales). RBI mandated 99.9% uptime SLA. Banks' legacy CBS (Core Banking Systems) couldn't handle load spikes of 40,000 TPS.

BA Solution Path: Juspay's hyperswitch architecture reroutes failed transactions across 12 bank rails in real-time. ML model predicts bank downtime 8 minutes ahead and pre-routes. BA-driven RCA (Root Cause Analysis) cut technical declines by 78%.

Key BA Findings

Each 1% improvement in success rate = ₹600 Cr in recovered transactions

Juspay processes 50% of all UPI transactions in India (as infrastructure)

BA insight: 40% of failures occur at PSP-to-bank handoff — not user end

Recommendations
BA Solution Framework: 6 Strategic Pillars

Data-backed recommendations for BFSI stakeholders — banks, fintechs, and regulators.

💳

Credit-on-UPI Monetisation

Enable banks to embed pre-approved credit lines in UPI flows. BA finding: 13.2 Mn eligible users represent ₹10,692 Cr annual revenue at 1.5% monthly yield.

📡

Rural Activation via UPI123Pay

Expand IVR-based payments to 150 Mn eligible rural users. ASHA workers, Jan Aushadhi stores, and PMKSY beneficiaries as beachhead segments.

🛡️

AI-Powered Fraud Prevention

Real-time behavioural biometrics + device fingerprinting can cut fraud 60%. BA recommendation: A federated ML model across PSPs without data sharing.

🌐

UPI Internationalisation

India–Singapore, India–UAE corridors live since 2023. Expand to 20 countries by 2027. ₹87,000 Cr inbound remittance market is addressable via UPI rails.

🏪

Merchant Super-App Strategy

WhatsApp's UPI integration unlocks 500 Mn merchants. BA insight: Merchants who use UPI for B2B payments have 3x higher retention than B2C-only users.

📊

Regulatory Sandbox Innovation

RBI's regulatory sandbox allows fintechs to test CBDC × UPI integration. First mover advantage in programmable payments is the next 5-year battleground.
